# Task Archive

Completed tasks, moved out of `task.md` once their Done-check passed and the work
shipped. `task.md` keeps only what is active, so it stays readable; everything
finished lives here with its evidence intact.

The `jarvis` skill appends an index row on completion and moves the block here.
